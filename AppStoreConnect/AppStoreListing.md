## Subtitle

Keep your Mac awake or let it sleep on a timer.

## Description

SleepLock is a native macOS menu bar utility that gives you quick control over system sleep behavior.

Use it to:
- keep your Mac awake for a fixed duration
- allow sleep again after a timer expires
- keep the Mac awake indefinitely until you turn it off
- launch automatically at login

SleepLock runs as a menu bar only app, uses native AppKit and SwiftUI, and is designed to stay lightweight.
